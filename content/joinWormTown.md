### Multiple paid research positions in the Dallas Lab

- Job description
  - You will work to clean, curate, augment, and georeference host-helminth data for the benefit of researchers wishing to understand parasite specificity, public health researchers thinking about potential spillover of helminths into humans or livestock, and biogeographers wishing to understand the distribution of biodiversity.
  - The majority of the work will involve finding and reading through the primary literature to find these data. 

- Job details
  - Pay: $15 / hour
  - Begins Jan 2027
  - You will be expected to work a minimum of five hours a week *on average*, although *when* you work and *how much* you work from week to week is up to you. You can optionally put in more hours than the minimum expectation. 
  - A one semester commitment is required at minimum. The position can be renewed for up to three years.  
  - Work is remote after initial training.
  - Students are invited (but not required) to also be involved in other research in the Dallas lab 
    - Interested students may conduct their independent research. Past students have presented at Discover USC, completed their senior thesis, and even published in peer-reviewed journals. 
    - Students will have access to lab resources such as powerful computers and lab study systems (*Daphnia* and flour beetles). 
  - Depending on student interests, we sometimes organize optional undergraduate-researcher-centered activities, such as [codeFest](/codefest), reading group, and special workshops. 

- To apply
    - Review a description of this [project](/about) and the [Dallas Lab](https://taddallas.github.io/), and skim over the titles in Tad's google scholar [profile](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=baoGwQ0AAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao) to get a flavor of what we do. 
    - Send the following materials to Vincent Pan (vpan@mailbox.sc.edu):
      - Your preferred name and preferred contact email address
      - Your CV
      - An unofficial transcript
      - A brief description of your interest in research and why you applied to this position (1-2 paragraphs max).
    - Applications received before November 15th will receive priority. 
    - Positions open until filled.
    - Short-listed candidates will be invited for an in person interview. 
    - Interview will take place between November 30th to December 4th. Invitation to an interview will be sent out over Thanksgiving week by the latest, so keep your eye out. 
    - Hiring decisions will be communicated by December 18th at the latest via email. 

- Eligibility
  - You must be a current student at the University of South Carolina.
